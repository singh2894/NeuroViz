03

WHAT'S IN THE BOX

BI

Auto dashboard

KPI cards and a chart grid the moment data loads. Click a bar to cross-filter everything; click once more to drill down a level.

BI

Chart builder + pivot

Bar, line, area, scatter, histogram, box, pie. Facet into small multiples, group or stack, pivot to a table, export CSV or PNG.

NLP

Ask in plain English

"Which region brings in the most money" becomes a ranked chart. Free LLM optional (Ollama, Groq); offline rules always work.

ML

Data diagnosis

Column types, target and task inference, class imbalance, skew, leakage suspects, correlation hotspots — before you model anything.

ML

One-click cleaning

Dedupe, impute, cap outliers, coerce "N/A" text to numbers — every action reported, output saved as a new dataset, original untouched.

ML

Model leaderboard

Logistic regression to random forests (XGBoost optional), cross-validated and ranked, with train/test metrics and permutation importance.

Runs on your machine. Your data goes nowhere.

Datasets live in memory for the session only — nothing is written to disk, nothing leaves your computer. Close the tab and it's gone. Free and open source under MIT; no account, no key, no upload.
